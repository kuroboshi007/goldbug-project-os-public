# Client-consumed Announcement Channels

An offer or CMS announcement is visible only after released clients implement its
feed contract, polling, filtering and display. Publishing cannot add support to an
installed client. Implement the integration once when needed, then routine campaigns
and latest-version notices can use the service without per-campaign code changes.

The active profile may enable an optional announcement channel and supply its
`how_to_url`; products explicitly choose used or not used during bootstrap. No
shared service is a universal dependency. Operational examples and private procedure
links remain in their owning product records or private profile.
